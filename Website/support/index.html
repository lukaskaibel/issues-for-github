Common questions

Do I need a GitHub account?

To work with your own issues, yes. To look around first, tap Explore with Sample Data on the first screen: two sample projects, stored on your device, where every feature works.

How do I sign in?

With Sign in with GitHub, or with a personal access token (classic) that has the repo, project and read:org scopes. Signed in on one of your devices, the others sign in by themselves through iCloud Keychain.

Why don't I see my issues?

Boards show the issues that are in a GitHub Project, with the project's Status field as their columns. The repositories your projects use are in the sidebar too, with all their issues: those on none of your boards are listed under No project, and one click puts them on a board. My Issues shows every open issue assigned to you, on a board or not.

An organisation's projects are missing.

Some organisations only allow apps their owners have approved. Ask an owner to approve Issues, or sign in with a personal access token that has access to the organisation.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every change shows at once and waits on the device until there is a connection, then goes to GitHub. When someone else changed the same text in the meantime, the edits are merged, and where they overlap you choose which to keep.

Why are there no notifications?

GitHub can only send notifications to an app through a server of the app's own, and Issues deliberately has none. Your devices refresh now and then in the background instead, and whenever you open the app.

How do I remove my data?

Sign Out removes the copy of your projects and issues from the device; Sign Out Everywhere also removes the login from iCloud Keychain. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. More in the privacy policy.

Does it cost anything?

No. Issues is free and open source; the code is on GitHub.

Issues is a client for GitHub Issues and GitHub Projects: people sign in to their own GitHub account to see and change their own projects. The app has no accounts of its own (Guideline 4.8 does not apply).

To review without a GitHub account, choose "Explore with Sample Data" on the first screen, on the Mac, iPhone and iPad. It opens two sample projects stored on the device; every feature works there and nothing is sent anywhere. To leave it, open the account menu at the top left and choose "Leave Sample Data".

"Sign in with GitHub" uses GitHub's device flow: the app shows a code to enter at github.com/login/device.

The Mac app runs in the App Sandbox and connects only to GitHub. On iPhone and iPad, background refresh updates the issues now and then while the app is closed.

Issues is a fast, native app for GitHub Issues and GitHub Projects, on the Mac, iPhone and iPad. It is calm to look at and quick to use, and everything stays in GitHub: teammates who don't use the app see the same issues and boards on github.com.

THE INBOX
• What changed for you, from GitHub's notifications: who assigned you, mentioned you, commented, asked for your review or closed an issue you follow
• Open one and the issue is right there, with what's new on top and the new comment marked
• Issues of yours that are due show up on the day, with Start, Mark as Done and Move to Tomorrow
• Read, archive and snooze with a key or a swipe; read and archived are the same on github.com
• Snoozed and unread ones stay in step on all your devices through iCloud

ON THE MAC
• Every project as a board or a list, plus My Issues with everything assigned to you
• Every issue of your repositories, also those on no board, which one click puts on one
• Drag issues to a new place, on the board or in the list, and shape the columns: add, rename, recolour and reorder them
• Keyboard first: J and K move between issues, S, P, A and L change status, priority, assignee and labels, D sets the due date, and ⌘K opens a command palette for everything else
• Select several issues and change them at once
• Peek at an issue with Space, or open it beside its properties, sub-issues and comments
• Sub-issues and blockers: put any issue under another, add existing issues as sub-issues, and mark what blocks what
• Descriptions drawn as on GitHub, with tables, checklists that tick and Mermaid diagrams
• Back and forward work as in a browser

ON IPHONE
• The Inbox as the first tab, with the number of unread notifications
• My Issues with everything assigned to you, grouped by status
• Every project and repository as a list, with sections that fold
• Swipe right to mark an issue done, swipe left to assign it to yourself or delete it
• Touch and hold an issue to change its status, priority, assignee, labels, parent or blockers, or drag it to a new place
• Search every project by title or number
• Tables, checklists and Mermaid diagrams in descriptions, drawn as on GitHub

ON IPAD
• A sidebar with all your projects and repositories, and the board with drag and drop
• The Inbox with the issue beside it, as in Mail
• Issues open beside their properties, as on the Mac
• With a keyboard: J and K to move, S, P, A and L to change an issue, ⌘N for a new one

DUE DATES THAT REMIND YOU
• Give an issue the day it is due: tomorrow, Friday, next week, a day in the calendar, or typed as "in 3 days"
• Cards and lists show it, orange on the day and red once it has passed
• On the day, your Mac, iPhone and iPad remind you, with buttons to start the issue, mark it done or move it to tomorrow
• The date is the project's Due date field on GitHub, so your team sees it too

FAST AND RELIABLE
• Every change shows at once and goes to GitHub in the background
• Works offline: changes wait and are sent when you're back online
• Careful with other people's work: when someone else changed the same text, nothing is overwritten

PRIVATE
• No account, no server, no tracking. Your login stays in your keychain and is only ever sent to GitHub.
• Signed in on one of your devices? The others sign in by themselves through iCloud Keychain.

Try it without an account: "Explore with Sample Data" opens two sample projects.

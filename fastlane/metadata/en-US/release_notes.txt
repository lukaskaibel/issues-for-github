The biggest update yet: plan with milestones, epics and a roadmap, open your project's views from GitHub, and use Issues in nine languages, in a new look built like Mail and Notes.

• Milestones: a board's next ones in the sidebar, each with its issues, how much is done and the day it's due. Complete one and move what's left to the next
• Epics: issues whose sub-issues are their work. On the board an epic's issues stand together under its name; drag the name to move them all
• A roadmap: your epics as bars over the milestones, by month or quarter. Drag a bar to move an epic and its issues to another milestone
• Your project's views from GitHub in the sidebar, as a board or a list, with filters as chips. Grouped by milestone or parent, a view shows how far each group is; a view of the current iteration shows its days left; a roadmap view is a timeline
• Milestones, views and the roadmap on iPhone and iPad too; the roadmap is a list on iPhone and a timeline on iPad
• A new look: on the Mac, the window of Mail and Notes in Liquid Glass, an inspector beside the issue, and right-click menus with a search field. On iPhone and iPad, issues with glass chips and comments as a conversation
• A new app icon, a board of cards, also on graphite or indigo, and an icon of your choice for each project
• Nine languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese, following your device's language
• The Inbox: your GitHub notifications, with the issue right beside them. Read, archive and snooze with a key or a swipe; github.com and your other devices agree
• Due dates with reminders: pick a day or type it, such as "in 3 days", and on the day your Mac, iPhone and iPad remind you
• Sub-issues hang under their parent; finish the last one and the parent is done too. Mark what blocks what
• Every issue, on a board or not: your repositories in the sidebar, with the issues on no board one click from going onto one
• Descriptions drawn as on GitHub: tables, checklists that tick, code and Mermaid diagrams
• Writing Markdown as in a notes app: bold, italics and code turn on and off, lists go on with Return, and on iPhone and iPad the bar above the keyboard has buttons for where the caret is
• Fewer clicks: picking a status, person or label closes the picker
• Fixed: turning an iPad to landscape could quit the app, and on the Mac a two-finger swipe no longer goes back by accident

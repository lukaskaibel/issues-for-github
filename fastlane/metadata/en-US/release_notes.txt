Undo for what you do, GitHub's issue types on every issue, and the board comes to iPhone.

• Undo and redo: ⌘Z takes back your last change and ⇧⌘Z brings it back, on the Mac and on iPad with a keyboard: status, fields, labels, parent, a card's place on the board, also for several picked issues at once
• Issue types: Bug, Feature, Task and your organisation's own show in their colour on cards, rows and the issue's page; click one or press T to change it
• An issue's fields from every project it is on, right in its details, and Add to project
• Drop an issue onto another one to make it a sub-issue: on boards and lists on the Mac, and on boards on iPhone and iPad
• Make a status right where you pick one, and give it an icon and a colour
• Put an issue into an iteration with a click or ⇧C, with the current and the next one marked
• Links to other issues in a description or a comment open them in the app
• My Issues as an overview, with a card for each project you work in
• All Issues in place of Repositories: a project's issues together with those on no board, also as a board
• Timelines zoom, and you drag a bar to move an issue's dates
• On the Mac, Search at the top of the sidebar, New Issue in the toolbar, and the peek in the middle of the window; on a Force Touch trackpad, press deeper to open it
• The board on iPhone: one column at a time, with every column and its count in a strip over it; touch and hold a card and drop it on a column in the strip
• Projects first: on iPhone the tab bar is gone and the app opens where you left off; pins put a view, a milestone or the roadmap next to the Inbox and My Issues
• On iPad, the sidebar lists every project, with the open one's views and milestones under it
• A quieter board: cards on grey without borders, labels as a dot and a name, and colour left for what needs you
• Sub-issues of sub-issues unfold as a tree, and a blocked issue names what blocks it, as #364 on its flag
• Pick several sub-issues and change them at once; pickers offer what you chose last
• Hide board columns or projects you don't need, on your device only
• Markdown drawn as on GitHub while you write it, with alerts, formulas, footnotes and pictures; typing ** starts bold and ## a heading
• Fixed: a new issue always finds a repository, labels recoloured on GitHub change in the app, and many smaller things

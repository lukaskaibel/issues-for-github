The board comes to iPhone, your projects come first on iPhone and iPad, and the board is quieter everywhere.

• The board on iPhone: one column at a time, with every column and its count in a strip over it. Swipe or tap the strip to turn to another column; touch and hold a card and drop it on a column in the strip
• Projects first: on iPhone the tab bar is gone, and the app opens where you left off. ‹ leads to the project's page, with All Issues, its views, its next milestones and the roadmap, and on to all your projects under the Inbox and My Issues
• Pins: swipe right on a view, a milestone or the roadmap, and it stands next to the Inbox and My Issues with how many of its issues are open
• On iPad, the sidebar lists every project, with the open one's views and milestones under it
• A quieter board: cards on grey without borders, labels as a dot and a name, and colour left for what needs you: a due date, an urgent issue, a blocker
• All Issues in place of Repositories: a project's issues together with those on no board, and how many of those are open
• Sub-issues of sub-issues unfold as a tree, listed by priority or in the order you pick
• A blocked issue names what blocks it, as #364 on its flag
• Pick several sub-issues and change them at once: ⌘-click and Shift-click on the Mac and iPad, Select on iPhone and iPad
• On the Mac, dragging one of several picked issues moves all of them
• Pickers offer the parents, epics, labels and people you chose last
• Hide board columns or projects you don't need, on your device only
• Markdown drawn as on GitHub while you write it, now with alerts, formulas, footnotes and pictures. Its marks show only next to the caret, and on iPhone and iPad the bar above the keyboard is glass, as in Notes
• Typing ** starts bold with its closing stars, and ## a heading; Tab and the arrow keys go from the title into the description and back
• On the Mac, long boards and the Inbox follow the pointer and scroll smoothly
• Fixed: My Issues says while your projects are still loading, dragging in the Mac's list works again, and many smaller things
